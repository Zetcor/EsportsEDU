RTS (Real-Time Strategy)

General Information Popular Games

Popular RTS Esports Games

StarCraft II
Blizzard Entertainment | July 27, 2010 | PC
StarCraft II is a sci-fi real-time strategy (RTS) game featuring three unique races, Terran, Zerg, and Protoss, each battling for galactic dominance. Renowned for its deep strategic gameplay, precise micro- and macro-management, and exceptionally high skill ceiling, it remains a pillar of competitive RTS esports.
Game Mechanics:
Players gather resources, construct buildings, and command armies in real time. Each race has distinct units, structures, and strategies, Terran with mobility and adaptability, Zerg with swarm tactics and map control, and Protoss with powerful but expensive units. Gameplay emphasizes multitasking, rapid decision-making, and strategic foresight.
Game Tournament:
Major competitive events include the Global StarCraft II League (GSL), the World Championship Series (WCS), and Intel Extreme Masters (IEM). These tournaments attract top players from around the globe and feature high-stakes matches broadcast live to international audiences.
Age of Empires II
Ensemble Studios | September 30, 1999 | Microsoft Windows Xbox
Age of Empires II is a historical real-time strategy game where players guide civilizations from the Dark Ages to the Imperial Age. The game focuses on managing economy, advancing technology, and leading military conquest across various unique civilizations.
Game Mechanics:
Players collect resources, build towns, advance through historical ages, and wage war using unique units and technologies specific to each civilization. Strategic decisions include managing economy, scouting opponents, and timing attacks or defenses.
Game Tournaments:
Major competitive events include Red Bull Wololo, Hidden Cup, and various community-driven tournaments. These events highlight top players and innovative strategies, keeping the game's esports scene vibrant and evolving.
Command & Conquer
Westwood Studios | August 31, 1995 | Microsoft Windows
Command & Conquer is a classic real-time strategy franchise set in alternate history and sci-fi universes. It is known for fast-paced gameplay, strategic base building, and memorable full-motion video cutscenes.
Game Mechanics:
Players harvest resources, construct bases, and deploy units in real-time battles. The series is famous for its asymmetric factions and accessible, action-oriented gameplay that appeals to both casual and competitive players.
Game Tournaments:
Though less prominent today, Command & Conquer had a significant competitive scene during the late 1990s and early 2000s. Community-driven tournaments continue to keep the spirit of competition alive.
