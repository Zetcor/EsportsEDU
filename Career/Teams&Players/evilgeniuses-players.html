Evil Geniuses PLAYERS

Max Mazanov AKA Demon1

Demon1

Role: Duelist

About

Max Mazanov from the USA is one of the standout players of Evil Geniuses, known for his sharp aim and clutch factor. As a key figure in the team’s success, his mechanical skill and poise under pressure set him apart. Demon1 joined Evil Geniuses and immediately proved himself as a clutch player, capable of making game-winning plays in high-pressure situations. His performances during the VCT Champions 2023, where he was awarded MVP, highlighted his ability to perform when the team needed him most.

Achievements

Demon1 was named the MVP of VCT Champions 2023, a testament to his exceptional skill and clutch performances. His ability to handle the most stressful moments with composure has made him one of the top players in Valorant.

Alexander Mor AKA Jawgemo

Jawgemo

Role: Controller

About

Alexander Mor from the USA is a skilled duelist and entry fragger, known for his aggressive playstyle and fast decision-making. His entry fragging helps set the tone for Evil Geniuses' aggressive strategies. Jawgemo’s fearless playstyle makes him a central figure in Evil Geniuses’ explosive pushes. His ability to open up rounds and secure early kills has been vital to the team's success. His aggressive nature and sharp mechanics have earned him a reputation as one of the top duelists in North America.

Achievements

Jawgemo’s entry fragging has been a key asset to Evil Geniuses, and his performances helped the team make a surprise run in VCT Champions 2023. His aggressive playstyle sets the pace for Evil Geniuses and allows them to take control early in rounds.

Kelden Pupello AKA Boostio

Boostio

Role: IGL

About

Kelden Pupello from the USA is the in-game leader (IGL) for Evil Geniuses, known for his leadership and motivational qualities. He is crucial in directing the team’s strategies and keeping morale high. Boostio’s leadership and ability to keep the team focused have been critical to Evil Geniuses' success. His strategic mind and calm under pressure helped guide the team to the VCT Champions 2023, where they made a deep run. Boostio’s influence as IGL extends beyond just tactics, he keeps the team motivated and united.

Achievements

As the IGL for Evil Geniuses, Boostio has guided the team to great success, including their impressive run in VCT Champions 2023. His leadership and ability to make key decisions under pressure have made him a standout player.
