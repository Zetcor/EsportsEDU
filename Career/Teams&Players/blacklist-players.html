Edward Dapadap AKA Edward

Edward

Role: EXP Lane

Team: Blacklist International

About

Edward Dapadap is an EXP laner from the Philippines known for his timing, resilience, and relentless frontline presence. A core part of Blacklist’s success since their M3 title, Edward provides crucial space for his teammates through proactive dives and great crowd control. He remains one of the most consistent EXP laners in the region.

Achievements

M3 World Champion; M5 3rd Place finisher; recognized for his clutch plays in high-stakes games.

Kiel Vj Cruzem AKA Owl

About

Kiel Vj “Owl” Cruzem is a gold laner from the Philippines known for his calculated aggression and strong positioning. Replacing long-time ADC OHEB, Owl stepped into a high-pressure role and proved himself during M5. His carry performances helped Blacklist remain competitive against the world’s best.

Achievements

M5 3rd Place finisher; breakout gold laner with strong playoff performances.

Kenneth Tadeo AKA Yue

Yue

Role: Mid Lane

Team: Blacklist International

About

Kenneth “Yue” Tadeo is a mid laner from the Philippines recognized for his cerebral gameplay and versatile mage pool. Yue became a reliable playmaker for Blacklist, controlling mid-lane tempo and setting up teamfights. His smart rotations and consistent utility play helped them secure key victories at M5.

Achievements

M5 3rd Place finisher; rising mid-lane talent with strong global presence.

Steve Imperial AKA Sensui

About

Steve “Sensui” Imperial is a jungler from the Philippines known for his objective-focused playstyle and calm under pressure. Sensui stepped up as Blacklist’s jungler with a steady hand, guiding early objectives and jungle vision setups. His presence allowed the team’s late-game-focused strategy to shine during M5.

Achievements

M5 3rd Place finisher; strategic jungler with growing international experience.

Dexter Alaba AKA Eson

Eson

Role: Support

Team: Blacklist International

About

Dexter “Eson” Alaba is a veteran support player from the Philippines praised for his leadership, communication, and tank play. A longstanding figure in Blacklist’s roster, Eson’s shotcalling and support play were pivotal in tough series during M5. He brought a wealth of experience to guide younger players on the team.
