Edward Dapadap AKA Edward

Edward

Role: EXP Lane

Team: Blacklist International

About

Edward Dapadap is an EXP laner from the Philippines known for his discipline, timing, and durable frontline impact. He consistently plays tanky heroes that open up space for his team. Edward is respected for his calm under pressure and strong laning fundamentals. Edward has been a key part of Blacklist International’s success since their M3 championship win. His ability to engage, absorb damage, and lock down opponents anchors the team’s playstyle. At both M3 and M5, he delivered clutch performances in high-stakes moments.

Achievements

M3 World Champion; M5 3rd Place finisher; recognized for his clutch plays in high-stakes games.

Kiel Vj Cruzem AKA Owl

About

Kiel Vj “Owl” Cruzem is a Filipino gold laner recognized for his calculated aggression and clean mechanics. Known for his sharp positioning, he excels at surviving while dishing out sustained damage. Owl brings a composed mindset to a high-pressure role. Owl made his international debut at M5, stepping up after replacing long-time carry OHEB. He impressed with consistent carry performances that helped Blacklist secure a top-three finish. His breakout run cemented him as one of the region’s rising gold lane stars.

Achievements

M5 3rd Place finisher; breakout gold laner with strong playoff performances.

Kenneth Tadeo AKA Yue

Yue

Role: Mid Lane

Team: Blacklist International

About

Kenneth “Yue” Tadeo is a mid laner from the Philippines known for his high game IQ and versatile mage pool. He plays a cerebral style centered around tempo control and teamfight setup. Yue’s presence gives Blacklist flexibility in draft and execution. Yue became a vital piece of Blacklist’s identity at M5 with his smart rotations and spell timing. His setups enabled clean teamfight wins and smooth map control. As a rising talent, he helped power the team to a 3rd place global finish.

Achievements

M5 3rd Place finisher; rising mid-lane talent with strong global presence.

Steve Imperial AKA Sensui

About

Steve “Sensui” Imperial is a Filipino jungler praised for his composed, objective-focused gameplay. He brings calm leadership to early-game setups and neutral control. His vision and map awareness support team-wide coordination. Sensui took over the jungle role for Blacklist and played a crucial part in their M5 performance. His steady tempo and vision work allowed the team’s carries to thrive. He remains one of the Philippines' most reliable and tactical junglers.

Achievements

M5 3rd Place finisher; strategic jungler with growing international experience.

Dexter Alaba AKA Eson

Eson

Role: Support

Team: Blacklist International

About

Dexter “Eson” Alaba is a veteran roamer from the Philippines known for his leadership, tank expertise, and communication. He brings strategic stability and experience to every roster he joins. Eson has long been a trusted voice in high-pressure matches. Eson returned to an active role at M5, providing key shotcalls and support setups during clutch games. His experience helped guide Blacklist’s younger talents on the global stage. A former M3 champion, he remains a respected veteran in the MLBB scene.

Achievements

M3 Champion (sub); M5 3rd Place as active support; respected leader in PH MLBB scene.
