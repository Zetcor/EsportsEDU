#TeamVitality

Location: North America

Region: USA

About

A premier French esports organization renowned for its dominance in CS2. With a legacy of championship wins, Vitality continues to set the standard for excellence in global esports. Their combination of experienced players and strategic depth makes them a top contender at any event.

History

Founded in 2013, Vitality has consistently been a top contender in global CS tournaments. Their combination of skilled players and tactical expertise has led them to multiple major championships. Team Vitality’s presence in the CS2 scene is marked by their adaptability and consistency.
