About

Legendary Korean team with unmatched legacy and skill. Their roster blends youthful talent with veteran leadership, creating a perfect balance of aggression and strategy. T1's ability to innovate metas while maintaining fundamentals makes them a constant threat. Their fanbase spans the globe, making every match feel like a home game.

History

T1 stands as the most iconic organization in League of Legends history. With three World Championships and countless LCK titles, they’ve defined excellence in competitive play. Their dominance spans over a decade, led by the ever-reliable Faker. Their 2023 Worlds victory marked a triumphant return to form, cementing their legacy as LoL's most storied franchise.

Meet the Players

T1’s roster is a powerhouse of legendary experience and emerging talent, united by an unwavering pursuit of excellence. Anchored by the iconic leadership of Faker, the team combines strategic brilliance, mechanical precision, and unmatched synergy. Each player brings a unique strength, whether it’s Doran’s resilience in the top lane, Oner’s sharp jungle instincts, or the dynamic bot lane duo of Gumayusi and Keria. Under the guidance of veteran coach kkOma, T1 continues to evolve while honoring its legacy. With every match, T1 reaffirms its status as a titan of competitive League of Legends.
