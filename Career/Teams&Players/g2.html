G2 TEAM

Overview

#G2ARMY

Location: Berlin, Germany

Region: Europe

About

Europe's most successful LoL team, known for bold plays. G2 combines Western creativity with world-class mechanics, creating an unpredictable, entertaining style. They're famous for pulling off unconventional strategies against favored opponents. Their confidence and adaptability make them Europe's consistent international representatives.

History

G2 Esports has dominated the LEC with their creative strategies and fearless style. With an MSI 2019 title and multiple European championships, they continue to set the standard for Western teams. Their confidence and flair have made them international fan-favorites and perennial threats.
