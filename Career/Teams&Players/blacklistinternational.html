BLACKLIST INTERNATIONAL TEAM

Overview

Location: Philippines

Region: Southeast Asia

About

Blacklist International is one of the most decorated teams in MLBB history, known for their “UBE” strategy—an ultimate bonding experience centered around tight unit play and sustainability. Representing the Philippines, their gameplay emphasizes team cohesion, objective control, and precise execution.

History

Blacklist rose to prominence with their world title at M3 and have remained consistent contenders ever since. At M5, they delivered another strong international showing, defeating top teams before falling in a lower bracket clash. Their disciplined teamfighting and veteran leadership secured them a top 3 finish.
