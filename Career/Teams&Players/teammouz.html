TEAM MOUZ

Overview

#MOUZ

Location: Germany

Region: Europe

About

A German esports organization known for nurturing young talent and consistent performances in international CS2 tournaments. MOUZ has built a reputation for developing future stars while competing at the highest level. The team's ability to combine emerging talent with experience has made them a formidable force in CS2.

History

Founded in 2002, MOUZ has a rich history in Counter-Strike, with a focus on developing emerging players into top-tier competitors. The organization has been consistently present in top tournaments, evolving with the competitive landscape. Their emphasis on growth and potential has made them a fan favorite in the CS2 community.
