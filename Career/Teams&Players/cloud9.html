CLOUD 9 TEAM

Overview

#C9

Location: California

Region: North America

About

NA's most consistent international contender. Cloud9 has built a reputation for identifying and developing North American talent while maintaining high competitive standards. Their proactive playstyle and willingness to innovate have made them the standard-bearer for the LCS region. The organization's culture of excellence continues to produce results year after year.

History

Multiple LCS titles; regular Worlds qualifier. Cloud9 is one of North America’s most successful organizations, known for developing top domestic talent and consistently performing at international events. Their combination of experienced veterans and young stars has kept them at the top of the LCS.
