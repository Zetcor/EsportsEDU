G2 PLAYERS

Sergen Çelik AKA BrokenBlade

BrokenBlade

Role: Top Lane

About

Sergen “BrokenBlade” Çelik is a German top laner known for his confidence, leadership, and deep champion pool. He plays a fearless, proactive style and often steps up in pressure moments. His vocal presence makes him a key shotcaller in team environments. BrokenBlade began his international career with TSM before becoming a core piece of G2 Esports. His aggressive laning and flexible picks have helped G2 win multiple LEC titles. He continues to anchor the top lane with consistency and leadership.

Achievements

A key figure in G2’s recent LEC titles, BrokenBlade is respected for his shotcalling and willingness to carry when needed.

Martin Sundelin AKA Yike

About

Martin “Yike” Sundelin is a Swedish jungler who burst onto the LEC stage with aggressive, tempo-focused gameplay. He’s known for bold pathing choices and creative early-game strategies. Despite being new to the scene, he brings confidence and flair to G2’s jungle. Promoted to G2’s starting roster in 2023, Yike quickly impressed with dominant early games and innovative champion picks. His performances helped G2 remain competitive in both domestic and international tournaments. He’s widely viewed as one of Europe’s rising jungle stars.

Achievements

As a standout LEC Rookie of the Year candidate, Yike showed rapid growth and helped stabilize G2’s jungle with impactful performances.

Rasmus Winther AKA Caps

Caps

Role: Mid Lane

About

Rasmus “Caps” Winther is a Danish mid laner renowned for his high-risk, high-reward playstyle and clutch mechanics. Nicknamed “Baby Faker,” he’s considered one of Europe’s greatest League of Legends talents. His explosive teamfighting and laning presence define his legacy. Caps has led G2 through multiple deep international runs, including a Worlds final and MSI championship. His playmaking is the centerpiece of G2’s style, often turning fights single-handedly. He remains a multi-time LEC champion and Europe’s strongest mid lane hope.

Achievements

A multiple-time LEC champion and Worlds finalist, Caps has consistently carried G2 with his creativity and playmaking ability.

Steven Liv AKA Hans Sama

Hans Sama

Role: Bot Lane

About

Steven “Hans Sama” Liv is a French ADC known for sharp mechanics and bold teamfight execution. He brings aggressive laning and crisp positioning to every match. His confident playstyle makes him a consistent threat in bot lane. After a stint in North America, Hans Sama returned to the LEC with G2, revitalizing their bot lane. His fast hands and fearless fighting have helped secure clutch wins. As one of Europe’s top ADCs, he remains a go-to damage dealer for G2.

Achievements

One of the top EU bot laners, Hans Sama has become a key damage dealer and is respected for his clutch performances on the big stage.

Mihael Mehle AKA Mikyx

Mikyx

Role: Support

About

Mihael “Mikyx” Mehle is a Slovenian support praised for his vision control, roaming, and synergy with teammates. He plays an intelligent, map-wide game that sets up success across lanes. His experience brings calm and clarity to chaotic moments. A former Worlds and MSI finalist, Mikyx continues to be a steadying force for G2. His setups and initiations consistently lead to major objectives and teamfight wins. His leadership and map sense are vital to G2’s ability to dominate early and mid-game.

Achievements

As an MSI finalist and veteran leader, Mikyx is renowned for initiating fights and making decisive plays in crucial moments.
