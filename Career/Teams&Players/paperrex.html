Paper Rex TEAM

Overview

#WGAMING

Location: Singapore

Region: Southeast Asia

About

Paper Rex is one of the most aggressive and flashy teams in the APAC region, known for their high-octane gameplay and bold strategies. Their roster thrives on synergy and explosive team plays, often catching opponents off guard with their unpredictable tactics. Consistently competing at an international level, Paper Rex has been a force in the APAC scene and continues to push the boundaries of competitive Valorant.

History

Representing the Southeast Asia region, Paper Rex quickly gained a reputation for their aggressive playstyle, often outplaying teams with fast-paced tactics. Their rise to prominence has been meteoric, with strong performances in VCT tournaments, including a top finish at VCT Masters. As a dominant force in APAC, Paper Rex continues to showcase the region’s competitive strength on the global stage.
