Blood Thirsty Kings PLAYERS

Michael Cosgun AKA MobaZane

MobaZane

Role: Core

About

Michael Cosgun is an American jungler known for his strong leadership and carry-focused playstyle. He rose to prominence through his strategic jungle pathing and high-impact decision-making. As a shotcaller, he's widely respected for his game sense and control over objectives. MobaZane captained BTK during their historic M3 run, guiding the team to a 3rd place global finish. His ability to adapt and draft signature picks played a key role in upsetting top-tier international teams. He remains a cornerstone of NA’s competitive Mobile Legends scene.

Achievements

M3 3rd Place; NA’s top jungler; team leader behind historic global performance.

Victor De Luna AKA Victor

About

Victor De Luna is an EXP laner from the U.S. known for his tanky, sustain-heavy play and versatility. His calm presence in the lane often absorbs pressure, allowing his team to set the tempo elsewhere. He’s valued for his reliability and low-risk style. As BTK’s frontline anchor, Victor delivered stable performances in high-stakes situations. His role in initiations and teamfight survivability helped balance the team’s aggressive tendencies. He was instrumental in BTK’s 3rd place showing at M3.

Achievements

M3 3rd Place, reliable frontline and initiator.

Ian Hohl AKA FwydChickn

FwydChickn

Role: Gold Lane

About

Ian "FwydChickn" Hohl is a flashy and mechanically gifted player from the U.S. who has switched between EXP and gold lane roles. His flexibility and timing in teamfights make him a fan favorite. Known for clutch moments, he thrives under pressure. FwydChickn’s adaptability was vital to BTK’s M3 success, shifting roles mid-tournament to meet team needs. His standout performances on both lanes showcased elite mechanics and decision-making. He helped power BTK’s breakthrough on the world stage.

Achievements

M3 3rd Place; multi-role star and top performer in NA’s breakout.

Ziad Kadhim AKA Zia

About

Ziad "Zia" Kadhim is a mid laner from the U.S. known for his steady hand and dependable mage play. His smart positioning and wave control support his team’s macro play. He’s a quiet contributor who anchors mid with discipline. Zia’s consistent laning and vision control gave BTK a strong central presence during M3. His clean zoning and safe style allowed his teammates to shine in aggressive roles. He played a key support role in the team’s international run.

Achievements

M3 3rd Place, consistent mid laner contributing to NA’s best world finish.

Mark Yocum AKA Shark

Shark

Role: Roam

About

Mark "Shark" Yocum is an American roamer who thrives on proactive map movement and aggressive setups. He’s known for his synergy with MobaZane and ability to read the game. His roaming keeps pressure on enemies while enabling allies to succeed. Shark’s rotations and initiations were crucial to BTK’s game flow during their M3 campaign. His shotcalling and engage timing helped them challenge top SEA teams. His playmaking was vital in the team’s deep international run.

Achievements

M3 3rd Place; roaming playmaker behind BTK’s historic upset run.
