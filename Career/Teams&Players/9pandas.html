9PANDAS Team

Overview

#9Pandas

Location: Eastern Europe

Region: Europe

About

9Pandas is an Eastern European team recognized for its aggressive playstyle and strong individual performances. Known for their fearless approach, they have become a formidable force in regional Dota 2 competitions. With a blend of experience and youth, 9Pandas is a team to watch on the international stage.

History

9Pandas made significant strides in 2023, with standout performances earning them a 3rd place finish at the Berlin Major. Their aggressive gameplay and individual talent quickly made them a team to reckon with, securing another 3rd place finish at TI12.
