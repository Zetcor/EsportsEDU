ONIC Esports TEAM

Overview

#itsONICtime

Location: Indonesia

Region: Southeast Asia

About

ONIC Esports is a leading Indonesian MLBB team known for their sharp early-game tempo, aggressive skirmishing, and tactical team coordination. Their high mechanical ceiling and disciplined map control made them strong contenders at M5. As MPL ID champions, they carried high expectations into the tournament and delivered a deep playoff run.

History

ONIC Esports has long been a dominant force in MPL Indonesia, claiming multiple titles and developing a reputation for cultivating elite talent. At M5, their synergy and fast-paced rotations helped them dismantle top-tier opponents en route to the Grand Finals. Despite falling short against AP Bren, their performance confirmed their place among the world’s elite.
