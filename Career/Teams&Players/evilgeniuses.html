Evil Geniuses TEAM

Overview

#Live evil, think free

Location: Seattle

Region: North America

About

Evil Geniuses made a massive splash at Valorant Champions 2023, emerging as one of the tournament's most impressive teams. Despite joining the competitive scene relatively late, their explosive gameplay and strategic depth propelled them to the top. With a roster full of talented players, Evil Geniuses is now considered one of the top teams in the North American region.

History

Evil Geniuses entered Valorant in 2021, quickly gaining attention for their rapid improvement and deep strategic approach. They made a surprise run to the VCT Champions 2023, finishing as one of the top contenders and securing their place among the best. Evil Geniuses has become a team to watch, with high expectations for their future performances.
