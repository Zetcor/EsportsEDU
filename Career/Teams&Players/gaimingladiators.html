#GaiminGladiators

Location: Europe

Region: Europe

About

Gaimin Gladiators is a European team known for their aggressive plays and adaptability. Their ability to quickly adapt to enemy strategies has made them one of the top teams in recent tournaments. With a well-rounded roster and strong coaching, Gaimin Gladiators are a fierce competitor in the Dota 2 scene.

History

Gaimin Gladiators made an impressive run through TI12, ultimately securing the runner-up spot. Their journey showcased their ability to adapt and execute under pressure. With a strong roster and a focus on aggressive team plays, they proved themselves as one of the top teams in the world.
