Team Mouz PLAYERS

David Čerňanský AKA Frozen

Frozen

Role: IGL

About

David "Frozen" Čerňanský is known for his steady rifling and calm leadership in high-pressure situations. As the in-game leader for Team Mouz, he plays a crucial role in shaping strategies and maintaining team coordination. His strong game sense and consistent performance have been instrumental to the team’s recent successes, including a strong showing in ESL Pro League Season 21.

Achievements

Frozen’s leadership and reliable rifling were key factors in MOUZ’s victory at ESL Pro League Season 21, establishing him as one of the top IGLs in the scene.
