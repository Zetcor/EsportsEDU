LOUD TEAM

Overview

#LOUD

Location: Brazil

Region: South America

About

LOUD is a top-tier Brazilian Valorant team renowned for their disciplined playstyle and aggressive execution. With a lineup full of veterans and emerging stars, LOUD has consistently dominated the Brazilian scene and proved their prowess on the international stage. Their comprehensive strategies and well-rounded gameplay made them the standout champions of VCT 2022.

History

LOUD’s rise to the top came with the VCT Champions 2022 title, where they showcased exceptional skill and synergy. A powerhouse in Brazil, they quickly became a formidable opponent in global competitions, consistently finishing at the top in regional events. Their reputation for adaptability and cohesive teamwork makes them one of the best teams in Valorant esports.
