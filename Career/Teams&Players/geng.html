About

The undisputed kings of Korea's LCK, Gen.G represents the pinnacle of disciplined, strategic League of Legends. Known for their methodical approach and flawless execution, they combine individual brilliance with impeccable team coordination. Their roster features some of the most respected veterans and exciting young talents in esports. As former world champions, they bring championship pedigree to every tournament. Gen.G's ability to adapt to any meta while maintaining their signature controlled style makes them perennial favorites.

History

Gen.G has dominated Korea's LCK with multiple domestic titles and a runner-up finish at MSI 2023. Their disciplined, structured playstyle makes them a global threat. With elite talent across all roles, they consistently compete at the highest level. Their strategic depth and veteran leadership set them apart as one of the world's best teams.

Meet the Players

G2 Esports’ roster is a formidable force, blending elite talent, relentless confidence, and a commitment to redefining the Western legacy in League of Legends. Led by the fearless leadership of Caps in the mid lane, the team thrives on creativity, coordination, and bold, game-changing plays. From Jankos' aggressive jungle pathing to BrokenBlade's top lane mastery, every player plays a vital role in G2's dynamic style. With Mikyx and Hans Sama anchoring the bot lane, the team showcases a perfect balance of mechanics and strategy, able to execute on both individual brilliance and flawless teamwork. Supported by a visionary coaching staff and a strong esports infrastructure in Berlin, G2 continues to set the pace for Western teams, with their eyes always on the next title.
