Team Vitality PLAYERS

Dan Madesclaire AKA apEX

apEX

Role: IGL

About

Dan "apEX" Madesclaire is known for his leadership skills and aggressive playstyle. As the in-game leader (IGL) of Team Vitality, he is integral to their tactical approach. His extensive experience in high-level CS:GO and CS2 competitions has shaped his ability to guide his team through intense moments. apEX’s leadership has been a cornerstone for Team Vitality’s success, especially during their championship runs. His strategic mind and clutch decision-making have earned him recognition in the esports community. He has led Vitality through multiple victories, including IEM Katowice 2025, further solidifying his status as one of the best IGLs in CS2.

Achievements

Led Vitality to multiple championships, including IEM Katowice 2025. Known for his strong leadership and resilience in critical moments, apEX’s tactical acumen has been crucial to Team Vitality’s rise.

Mathieu Herbaut AKA ZywOo

ZywOo

Role: Rifler / AWPer

About

Mathieu "ZywOo" Herbaut is one of the most talented CS:GO and CS2 players of his generation. His exceptional skill as a rifler and AWPer has made him a key player for Vitality. Known for his calm demeanor, ZywOo’s game sense and mechanical prowess have allowed him to dominate in the global scene. ZywOo’s rise to the top began with his breakthrough performances in 2018, and he quickly became one of the best players in the world. His clutch plays and consistency have helped Vitality win major tournaments, including IEM Katowice 2025. ZywOo’s ability to adapt to various in-game situations and his unmatched precision with rifles have cemented his place as a superstar.

Achievements

Secured 24 HLTV MVPs, surpassing s1mple's record for MVP awards. His instrumental role in Vitality’s victories, especially in 2025, showcases his elite status in the CS2 scene.

Shahar Shushan AKA flameZ

flameZ

Role: Entry Fragger

About

Shahar "flameZ" Shushan is known for his aggressive playstyle and strong rifling skills. He plays a pivotal role in Vitality’s strategies, often taking on the role of an entry fragger. His sharp decision-making and precision under pressure have earned him a reputation as one of the top up-and-coming players in CS2. flameZ joined Team Vitality in 2022, quickly becoming a reliable contributor to their success. His ability to perform in high-stakes matches has made him an invaluable asset, particularly in aggressive pushes and securing map control. flameZ has shown his mettle by consistently delivering standout performances.

Achievements

Key rifler contributing to Vitality's strategic plays. His aggressive entry fragging and impact in high-pressure moments have been essential to the team’s victories in top tournaments.

William Merriman AKA Mezii

Mezii

Role: Versatile

About

William "Mezii" Merriman is a versatile player known for his ability to clutch in critical moments. His calm and calculated approach to the game has helped him excel in high-pressure situations. A well-rounded player, mezii’s adaptability is a major asset to Team Vitality. With experience in multiple roles, mezii’s flexibility and ability to shine in clutch moments have made him an indispensable part of Vitality’s roster. His contributions have been key in several high-profile matches, and he continues to perform consistently on the international stage.

Achievements

Known for his clutch situations and his contributions to Vitality’s tactical execution. mezii’s versatility has been crucial to Vitality’s success in tournaments like IEM Katowice 2025.

Robin Kool AKA Ropz

Ropz

Role: Rifler / AWPer

About

Robin "ropz" Kool is known for his strategic playstyle and technical skill with both rifles and the AWP. As one of the most consistent players in CS:GO and CS2, ropz has been a key part of Vitality’s success in major tournaments. His strong map awareness and positioning are his standout traits. Ropz has an extensive career, marked by his time with FaZe Clan before joining Vitality in 2024. His experience, especially during their Grand Slam win, and his previous victory in PGL Major Antwerp 2022 with FaZe Clan, add a wealth of knowledge to Vitality.

Achievements

Contributed to Vitality's Grand Slam win and previously won PGL Major Antwerp 2022 with FaZe Clan. ropz’s experience and technical skill have been essential in Vitality’s victories.
