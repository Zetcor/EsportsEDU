BLOOD THIRSTY KINGS TEAM

Overview

#BloodThirstyKings

Location: North America

Region: USA

About

BTK was a North American underdog team that shocked the world at M3 with raw mechanics, fearless drafts, and unorthodox strategies. Despite lacking a major organization or coaching staff, they thrived on synergy and self-discipline. Their Cinderella story inspired fans globally.

History

BTK advanced through the group stage and upset top teams like EVOS SG and Blacklist in the playoffs. Though they lost to ONIC PH in the lower bracket final, their 3rd-place finish remains one of the best NA showings in MLBB history.

Meet the Players

Blood Thirsty Kings (BTK) is a professional esports team based in North America, known for their exceptional skills and teamwork in the Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB) scene. The team gained recognition for their impressive performance in various tournaments, showcasing their dedication and passion for the game. With a roster of talented players, BTK has made a significant impact in the esports community, earning a loyal fanbase and respect from competitors. Their journey in the competitive gaming landscape continues to inspire aspiring players and fans alike.
