Cloud9 PLAYERS

Ibrahim Allami AKA Fudge

Fudge

Role: Top Lane

Team: Cloud9

About

Ibrahim Allami from Australia is known for his versatility and leadership in the top lane. Born in 2002, he transitioned from OPL to LCS and quickly became one of NA's most reliable players. His positive attitude and work ethic make him a locker room leader. Fudge transitioned from top-tier Oceania play to becoming a pillar of Cloud9’s success. His flexibility to play carries or tanks makes him a reliable component of team strategy. He is known for stepping up in playoff situations and adapting quickly.

Achievements

2022 LCS Championship winner. Two-time LCS All-Pro Team selection (2021, 2022). Maintains one of the highest CS differentials (+12.3 at 15min) among LCS top laners.

Robert Huang AKA Blaber

About

Robert Huang is an American jungler famous for his explosive early game and dominance in the LCS. The California native stands at 183cm and is known for his aggressive playmaking. His journey from Cloud9 Academy to MVP is an NA success story. Blaber has been with Cloud9 since his rookie year and is a multi-time MVP who thrives under pressure. His aggressive, high-risk style sets the tone for the team. He remains a defining player in North American League of Legends.

Achievements

Two-time LCS MVP (2020 Summer, 2021 Spring). Four-time LCS champion (2019-2022). Holds record for most First Blood participations (84) in LCS history.

Joseph Pyun AKA Jojopyun

Jojopyun

Role: Mid Lane

Team: Cloud9

About

Joseph Pyun is a Canadian mid laner known for his confidence, personality, and raw mechanical talent. At just 19, he's already one of the most exciting players in the LCS. His Korean heritage influences his disciplined approach to improvement. After gaining popularity on Evil Geniuses, Jojopyun joined Cloud9 to bring fresh energy to the roster. His fearless laning and trash talk made him a fan-favorite. He represents the next generation of NA mid lane stars.

Achievements

2022 LCS Rookie of the Year and Championship winner. Youngest player to reach LCS finals at age 17. Maintains one of highest damage shares (28.7%) among NA mid laners.

Kim Min-cheol AKA Berserker

About

Kim Min-cheol from South Korea is one of the LCS's brightest ADC stars. The 21-year-old brings world-class mechanics from his time in T1's system. His quiet professionalism contrasts with his explosive in-game playstyle. Berserker joined Cloud9 from T1’s development system and immediately dominated the LCS bot lane. His mechanics and teamfighting quickly made him a top-tier carry. He is widely viewed as the future of the LCS ADC role.

Achievements

2023 LCS First Team All-Pro and Championship winner. Holds record for highest KDA (12.3) in LCS playoff history (2023 Spring). Consistently leads LCS in late game damage percentage (34.2%).

Jesper Svenningsen AKA Zven

Zven

Role: Support

Team: Cloud9

About

Jesper Svenningsen from Denmark is a veteran of both the ADC and support roles in the LCS and EU LCS. Now 26, his experience provides stability to Cloud9's bot lane. His analytical mind contributes to the team's strategic planning. Zven transitioned from star ADC to support in Cloud9, bringing shotcalling and game sense to the role. His deep knowledge of both roles enhances his synergy with Berserker. He’s one of the most experienced and flexible players in the region.

Achievements

Two time EU LCS Champion (2016, 2017) and 3-time LCS Champion (2020-2022). One of only three players to win titles in both EU and NA. Holds record for most international appearances (7) by a Western player.
